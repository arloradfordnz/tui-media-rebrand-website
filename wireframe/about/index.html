About

The person behind the camera

A one-person video studio in Nelson, run by Arlo Radford.

Kia ora, I'm Arlo. I started Tui Media, I'm 17, and I'm still at Nelson College. I grew up with a camera in my hands and I've been doing this properly for more than four years.

I make video content and ads for local businesses, and I do every part of it myself, from the first call to the final edit. Nothing gets lost between people, because there aren't any.

Most clients are on a monthly plan, where one shoot becomes a month of reels, photos and posts. Others come to me for a single ad project, set up in their own ad account.

So far that's more than thirty Nelson businesses, plus a project with Nikon New Zealand for the launch of one of their cinema cameras.
