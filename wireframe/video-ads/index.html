How it works

From our first call to your ads going live in about three weeks.

20 minutes

Fit call

A quick call to see if we're a fit. No pressure.

Week one

Strategy

We get to know your business, who your customers are and what's going to get them to reach out. Then we lock in a plan together.

Week one

Script

We write the script, the hooks and the shot list so everyone knows what's being filmed.

One day on site

Film

We bring our own gear. We shoot wide as well as vertical, so it works everywhere it runs.

Week two

Edit

We cut a few versions with different hooks and sizes, so each one suits where it's running.

Week three

Launch

We set up the campaign in your ad account and launch it.

Week three

Handover

You get the footage, the ads and the ad account. It's all yours.

What you keep

Once the project wraps up, it's all yours. We don't hang onto anything or lock you out of your own account.

The raw footage

Every single take from the shoot, outtakes and b-roll included, in a folder with your name on it. We keep a copy of the raw files for 6 months too.

The final ads

Cut to fit reels, feed, square and in-stream, so nothing ends up awkwardly cropped.

The ad account

Set up in your name before a cent gets spent. It stays with you, along with all the data it's built up.
