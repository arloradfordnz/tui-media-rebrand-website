How it works

From the first call to a live campaign in about three weeks.

20 minutes

Fit call

A quick call to see if we're a fit. No pressure.

Week one

Strategy

We look into your business, who you're trying to reach and what's likely to get them to act. Then we agree on a plan together.

Week one

Script

We write the script, the hooks and the shot list so everyone knows what's being filmed.

One day on site

Film

We bring our own gear. We shoot wide as well as vertical, so it works everywhere it runs.

Week two

Edit

We cut several versions with different hooks and formats, so each one fits where it's running.

Week three

Launch

We set up the campaign in your ad account and launch it.

Week three

Handover

You get the footage, the ads and the ad account. It's all yours.

What you keep

When an ad project ends, everything is yours. No files held back, no account you can't log into.

The raw footage

Every take from the shoot, including outtakes and b-roll, in a folder in your name. We keep a copy of the raw files for 6 months too.

The final ads

Cut for reels, feed, square and in-stream, so nothing gets badly cropped.

The ad account

Set up in your name before any money is spent. It stays with you, along with everything it's learned.
