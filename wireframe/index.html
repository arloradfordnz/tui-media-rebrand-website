  • You want leads, enquiries or bookings, not followers

The process we follow

20 minutes

Fit call

A quick call to check it's actually a fit, before either of us spends anything.

Week one

Strategise

We look into the business properly, who you are and who you're trying to reach, then build a plan around what'll actually bring in customers.

Week one

Script

We write the script and shot list, the real plan for what gets filmed.

One day on site

Film

Shoot day. We bring our own gear and shoot wide and horizontal, not just phone-style vertical clips.

Week two

Edit

Cut into a few versions, different hooks, different formats, so it fits properly wherever it runs instead of getting cropped after the fact.

Month one

Launch and optimise

Ads go live on Meta. We check in and adjust daily for the month. What's working keeps running, what's not gets cut.

End of month one

Handover

At the end of the month, you get the keys. Raw footage, final ads, and the ad account, all yours.

What you get, and what you keep

At the end of the month, everything gets handed over. No files held back, no account you can't log into, nothing to come back and ask for later.
