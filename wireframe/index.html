Most video gets made to look good. This gets made to bring you work. It's for the owner who is tired of quoting people who were never going to say yes.

It's not for everyone

We take on a handful of clients at a time, so the fit has to be right before either of us spends a dollar.

Who this is for

  • Want leads, enquiries, or bookings, not followers or views
  • Have the margin to fund ad spend on top of the project fee
  • Understand that ads take testing, and results aren't instant
  • Believe in what they're selling

Who this isn't for

  • Want a guarantee before anything has been tested
  • Need to sign off on every shot, caption and change along the way
  • Are shopping on price and want the cheapest quote you can find
  • Expect enquiries without real budget behind the ads

The process we follow

20 minutes

Fit call

A short call to work out whether ads make sense for you at all. If they do not, we say so and you have lost twenty minutes.

Week one

Strategise

We work out who's actually buying, what stops them, and which offer is worth putting money behind. That decides everything after it.

Week one

Script

Concepts and hooks written against those objections. The first two seconds decide whether the rest gets watched, so that's where the work goes.

One day on site

Film

One day on site, filmed so it can be cut multiple ways. You don't lose a week of your team's time to a video.

Week two

Edit

Cut for the platform it's running on, in the formats you need, with the hooks swapped so there's more than one version to test.

Month one

Launch and optimise

Live, then watched. We keep what's working, cut what isn't, and recut against the numbers for the first month.

End of month one

Handover

Every file, the ad account, and everything it has learned goes to you. Nothing is held back and there is nothing to ask for later.

What you get, and what you keep

At the end of the month, everything is handed over. No files held hostage, no account you can't log into, nothing you have to come back and ask for later.

The raw footage

Every take from the shoot goes up to a portal we set up in your name. Not just the ones that made the final cut, the outtakes and b-roll too, and they stay there for you to pull down whenever you need them.
