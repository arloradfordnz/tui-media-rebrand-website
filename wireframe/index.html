How an ad project works

Monthly content runs the same way without the ad launch. We plan the month, we film, we edit, we deliver.

20 minutes

Fit call

A quick call to see if we're a fit. No pressure.

Week one

Strategy

We look into your business, who you're trying to reach and what's likely to get them to act. Then we agree on a plan together.

Week one

Script

We write the script and shot list so everyone knows what's being filmed.

One day on site

Film

We bring our own gear. We shoot wide as well as vertical, so it works everywhere it runs.

Week two

Edit

We cut a few versions with different hooks and formats, so each one fits where it's running.

Month one

Launch and tweak

Ads go live on Meta. We check in and adjust daily for the month. What works keeps running, what doesn't gets cut.

End of month one

Handover

You get the raw footage, the final ads and the ad account. All yours.

What you keep

When an ad project ends, everything is yours. No files held back, no account you can't log into.
